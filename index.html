Thames Sea Level Rise Pathways (Greater London)

Decadal line offsets on both banks based on AR6 scenario projections for Tower Pier (PSMSL 336).

How to use

  • Move Scenario slider to switch SSP pathways.
  • Move Percentile slider to switch low / median / high estimate.
  • Move Offset scale slider to control line spacing.
  • Click Download CSV to export all generated left/right pathway lines.
